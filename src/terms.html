4. Data and analytics notice

What we collect when you have an account:

  • Account details: your name, email address, organisation, country and how you plan to use OBQ.
  • Sign-in records: the time of each sign-in, the sign-in method, your interface language and your browser or device type.
  • Quiz set-up records: each time you start a quiz we store information about the set-up: the event name, your name and organisation, the date, the number of participants, the quiz language, the books or scope chosen, the quiz mode and style, the question source, the interface language, the number of questions and the start time. This helps us count and understand how OBQ is used.
  • Saved items: if you are signed in online, your saved quizzes and question bank may be stored with your account so you can use them on other devices.

Analytics (your agreement is included in these Terms): by accepting these Terms you agree that we may collect anonymous usage analytics with Google Analytics for Firebase: for example which screens are used, how many quizzes are started and finished, and which languages and scopes are chosen. We use this only to improve OBQ and to report on how it is used. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings. Turning it off does not stop the account details, sign-in records and quiz set-up counts described above, which are needed to run the service.

What stays on your device: the names of participants and their points, your results history, your Bible texts, your settings and, if you use it, your own Firebase connection details are kept on your device. They are not sent to us. They are sent to a Firebase project only if you connect your own project and choose to send results there.

Device storage: the app stores information in your browser (for example your settings, saved quizzes and sign-in session) so that it works and remembers your choices.
