Everything you need to prepare, run and finish an Oral Bible Quiz. Tap a heading to jump to it.

Quick start

  1. Sign in, or create an account the first time. Leave Remember me on this device ticked on your own phone or computer: you then stay signed in until you press Sign out. After signing in you land on the Home page. After you create an account, open the confirmation email we send to your address, click the link, then come back and press I have confirmed my email. Look in the spam folder if you do not see it, and use Send the email again if needed.
  2. Press Set up a quiz (or OBQ Lite - Quick Quiz for almost no setup) and fill in your name, the event name, the date, the number of participants and the languages. Choose Individuals or Groups, the Quiz style (Normal or Simple), the scope (1 book, 2 books or more than 2), the number of questions and where the questions come from.
  3. Press Start quiz. The app goes into full screen.
  4. For each question: say the question, wait for answers, press Show correct answer, type the names of those who answered correctly, press Give points, then Next question.
  5. At the end save the results as PDF or CSV.

New to this? Press Try the 5-question sample quiz on the setup page and run through it once alone, or choose the Simple quiz style for your first real event.

The Home page and OBQ Lite

The Home page opens after sign-in and whenever you press Home. It has an introduction on the left and the menu on the right in four groups: Run a quiz, one Quiz Builder box (your Question Bank and Bible Knowledge Base), one Quiz Results box (My Quiz Results and My Firebase Backup), and Account and help (Analytics Dashboard, Settings and these instructions). Below them is the Your events panel (your next scheduled events with a Start now button, your recent quizzes, a few numbers, a Getting started checklist and a daily tip), and then the Verse of the Day (in your interface language if you have loaded a Bible text in it, otherwise the King James Version). If your organisation has published a notice (for example an event), it appears at the top. In Run a quiz the first row is OBQ Lite - Quick Quiz and Demo Contest, the second row is Set up a quiz and My saved quizzes.

OBQ Lite - Quick Quiz is the fastest way to run a quiz. Choose the quiz language, the part of the Bible (whole Bible, New Testament, Old Testament or one book), the number of questions and Individuals or Groups, then press Start quiz. It always uses the Simple style: regular and multiple-choice questions, 1 point each, with no clues, no 50:50 and no multi-part. The Demo Contest runs a ready-made 10-question quiz so you can see every kind of question.

Terms and Conditions, and Settings

When you create an account you read and accept the Terms and Conditions in a pop-up (the I accept button works after you scroll to the end). The terms include the notice about data and anonymous analytics. You accept them once, when you create your account, and you can read them again in Settings.

The Settings button in the top bar opens a screen that starts with your profile (name, organisation, country and how you use OBQ), which you can change and save with Update profile; your email address is your sign-in and cannot be changed there. Below it you can choose a dark, light or device-matching theme, the font, the text size (small to extra large), whether to play sounds, the read-aloud speed, whether to keep the screen awake during a quiz, and whether to share anonymous analytics. The choices are remembered on your device (the analytics choice is also saved with your account).

The logo and name at the left lead back to Home. Working offline and staying signed in

  • Install the app: press Install OBQ on this device on the Home page or in Settings, Install and offline use (or use the browser menu: Install app, or Add to Home Screen; on an iPhone, Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it once with internet. The installed app opens like any other app and runs offline. After that the app itself opens without internet, and your saved quizzes, question bank, Bible texts and results are on your device.
  • The first sign-in needs internet if your organisation uses online accounts, because the password is checked online. With Remember me on this device ticked, the device stays signed in until you sign out, and the same email and password also work later without internet. Do not tick it on a shared computer. In Settings you can Forget this device.
  • Local accounts (no online service) work without internet at any time, but exist only in that browser.
  • Without internet these do not work: sending results to your Firebase, cloud sync, Bible Brain downloads and usage counting. Running a quiz, the PDF and CSV, the timer, and read aloud (with an installed voice) all work.

Before the event (checklist)

  • Questions: decide the source: the built-in English questions, your own Excel question bank, or questions made from Bible text in your language (see "Your questions" below). Preview a few so you know what to expect.
  • Save it: on the setup page press Save for later. Save as a Template to reuse it for other events, or as a Scheduled event with the date. Tick Freeze the questions now if you want exactly the same questions on the day. Saving clears the setup form (your name is kept) and puts the quiz in My saved quizzes at the top of the setup page. There press Start now to run it, or Open in setup to change something first. A green line reminds you of events in the next 7 days (only while the app is open).
  • Test: run the sample quiz once on the same device and screen you will use, with your presentation remote if you have one (Settings, "Test your presentation remote").
  • Device: charge it, switch off notifications, turn the sound up (the app gives a short sound for the timer and answers), and install the app on the home screen if you can (browser menu, Install app). Installed, it also works without internet.
  • Do not reload the page during a quiz. Scores are kept only while the quiz is open. Reloading ends it and the points are lost.
  • Projector or TV: use landscape. The app fits each question on one screen.

Running the quiz

The app goes into full screen when the quiz starts (the browser asks for a tap or click first, so this happens when you press Start). To leave full screen press Esc or click the button in the top bar that says "Full screen: press Esc or click here to exit". Press it again to return. On an iPhone full screen is not available; use the installed app instead.

The screen. The coloured bar at the top shows the questions (green = done, orange = now). On the left: the question number, the points, the timer and the question. On the right: the step guide, the names box, the Next button and the points table.

The same four steps for every question. A bar above the names box always tells you which step you are on.

  1. Say the question aloud and let people answer. Press START if you want the timer (30 seconds, or 45 for multi-part). When time is up the question hides, so everyone looks at you.
  2. Press Show correct answer. The answer appears in green with the Bible reference and a short note to read aloud.
  3. Type the names of those who answered correctly and press Give points (or press Enter). Separate several names with commas, or tick names from "Earlier correct answers". Each name can score once per question. If a point is given by mistake, press the x next to the name to remove it.
  4. Press Next question. It stays dim until the answer is shown and points are given. Nobody answered correctly? Press Give points with the names box empty; it records that nobody answered and unlocks Next.

Read aloud. The Read aloud button (or the R key) reads the current question, its options and any clues out loud with the voice of your device, and reads the answer once it is shown. Tick Read each question aloud automatically on the setup page or OBQ Lite and every new question, clue, hint and 50:50 is read for you. It needs a voice for the quiz language: phones and computers include voices for many Indian languages, but not all; if none is found, OBQ tells you, and you can add a voice in the device's text-to-speech settings. Turn the volume up and use a speaker for a group.

Read aloud in your language. OBQ uses the voices installed on your device, so the quality depends on the device. When the quiz language is not English:

  • Choose a voice. On the setup page or OBQ Lite, tick Read each question aloud. A Read aloud voice list appears with the voices found for your quiz language (the best one is picked automatically). Press Test voice to hear it. Your choice is remembered for that language.
  • No voice found? OBQ then does not read the question, because a wrong voice would be unintelligible. For Marathi and Nepali, OBQ can use a Hindi voice if there is one (same script). Otherwise install a voice: Android: Settings, System, Languages and input, Text-to-speech output, Google Speech Services (or Samsung text-to-speech), Install voice data, then pick the language. Windows 11: Settings, Time and language, Speech, Manage voices, Add voices. Microsoft Edge also offers free online "Natural" voices for many Indian languages and is usually the best choice on a computer. iPhone, iPad, Mac: Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices. (Menu names differ a little between devices.)
  • Odia, Punjabi and some others have few voices on most devices. If none exists, read the questions yourself, or use a device that has one.
  • Long questions are read in short pieces, and option letters are spoken in your language (for example "ए, बी, सी, डी" in Hindi). Turn the volume up and use a speaker for a group.

The grey buttons are not broken: hover over them (or tap them) and a tip explains what is missing. The arrow button goes back to the previous question; if points were not given yet it asks you to press it twice. The Home button leaves the quiz and also asks you to press twice.

After the quiz

  • The final screen shows the winner(s) and the full points table, with confetti if someone scored.
  • Save results as PDF: an A4 report with your name, email, organisation, event, date, participants, quiz language, scope, quiz style, number of questions and the points table.
  • Download results (CSV): the same results for a spreadsheet, one row per participant. A name that starts with = + - or @ gets an apostrophe in front so it cannot run as a formula.
  • Every finished quiz is also kept in Quiz Results, My quiz results on this device, where you can download or delete it.
  • Optional backup: on the Quiz Results page you can connect your own Firebase and send results there. (create a project, allow anonymous sign-in, create the database, publish the rules, paste the settings). OBQ's own cloud never receives points or names; it only counts that a quiz was set up.
  • Save this quiz to redo later stores the quiz you just played, with its exact questions, in My saved quizzes. Press Start now there to run the same quiz again with fresh scores.
  • New quiz and Home return to the setup page. After a finished quiz the setup form starts empty (your name and the interface language are kept).

Your questions

  • Built-in: about {{BANK_COUNT}} English questions across all 66 books, with every kind of question. Narrow scopes may run short, and the app tells you.
  • My question bank (Excel): Quiz Builder, Download Excel template, fill in the Questions sheet (see the Examples sheet), save, then Upload question bank. Rows with a problem are skipped and listed with their row number. Tick "Add to my existing bank" to add instead of replacing. Categories: One answer, Multiple choice, Short answer with hints, Clue based, Multi-part. Optional columns: Level (Regular, Trivia, Difficult), Points, Seconds, Intro.
  • My Bible text: Quiz Builder, Bible Knowledge Base. Choose the language, then upload USFM, USFX, USX or text files (or a zip of them), import a ready-made bundle, or use Bible Brain (more information at biblebrain.com): first request your free API key (it is sent to your email), then enter it, press Load countries and languages, choose a country and a language, and press Download next to a fileset in the list. The list shows only the USX and USFM filesets and tells you how many other text filesets (for example plain text) were found but are not shown. You can also type a fileset ID yourself. Bible Brain downloads work from your own web address, not from the preview link. Press Preview questions to check samples, then choose the source Questions generated from my Bible text on the setup page. These questions are "complete the verse" and "which book is this verse from". Have a native speaker review them.
  • Quiz language and bank language: the app uses only questions that match the quiz language chosen on the setup page (or have no language set).
  • Licences: make sure you may use each Bible version you load.

Tips for oral groups

  • Read each question twice, slowly. Read options as "A ... B ... C ... D".
  • Keep to a rhythm: question, think time, answer, names, points. Say the points out loud so everyone hears the score.
  • Use the answer note. After the answer, read the short "More to know" line: it teaches as well as scores.
  • Make helpers: ask a helper to call out the group names so you can type quickly.
  • Start Simple for children, new groups or noisy rooms. Move to Normal when everyone knows the routine.
  • Check the quiz language and have a native speaker review generated or uploaded questions before the event.

If something goes wrong

  • The Next question button is grey. Show the correct answer and give points first (or give points with no name if nobody answered).
  • I gave points to the wrong person. Press the x next to the name under "Correct answer given by". The points are removed.
  • I pressed Back by mistake. The quiz keeps your points. Press the Next question button to continue.
  • Full screen does not start. The browser only allows it after a click. Click the full screen button in the top bar. On an iPhone it is not available: install the app to the home screen. Press Esc to leave.
  • The timer has no sound. Check the Sound button on the quiz screen and your device volume.
  • My remote does nothing. Click once on the page first so it has focus, then test the buttons in the box in Settings. Some remotes need their USB receiver plugged in.
  • Not enough questions. Choose more books, a wider scope, another source, or upload a bigger question bank.
  • The page reloaded and the scores are gone. A quiz cannot be recovered after a reload. Next time keep the page open; the final results are saved only when the quiz ends.
  • Excel upload shows skipped rows. Read the row numbers and reasons on screen, correct those rows in Excel and upload again.
  • "Email or password is incorrect" although I am sure it is right. First look at the note under the sign-in box. Local mode: the account exists only in the browser and website address where you created it, so it is not found in another browser, on another device, after the browser data was cleared, or on a different web address (for example localhost instead of the published site). Online accounts: an account made in local mode, or before the site was connected online, does not exist online: create an account with the same email (your saved quizzes on this device use your email, so they appear again), or use "Forgot password" or "Email me a sign-in link". If you see "Cannot reach the online sign-in service", wait for the internet connection and press Try again.
  • I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen use "Forgot password?" (available when your organisation has turned on online sign-in).

Privacy

Participant names and points stay on your device (and in your own Firebase only if you connect one). Do not share a device with your results history. Delete finished results you no longer need in the Quiz Builder. Tell participants that their names and scores are recorded.
